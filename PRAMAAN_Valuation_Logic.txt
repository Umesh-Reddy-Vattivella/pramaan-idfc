1. OVERVIEW AND PRINCIPLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PRAMAAN values a property offered as security for a Loan Against Property (LAP)
without a physical site visit. The bank officer:
  - opens the case with the facts from the loan file (and, optionally, what the
    customer paid for the property and what they claim it is worth),
  - collects documents from the loan file and from the customer,
  - confirms the location and draws the plot boundary on the satellite map,
  - inspects the property on a live video call with the customer at the site,
  - takes each floor's area from the floor plan and checks the total against
    the building's roof outline on the satellite top view,
  - adopts the official government value (Telangana) or links to the state
    portal, and fetches market evidence around the property,
  - lets the engines compute the rate, the value, the loan and the checks,
  - issues a signed-off technical valuation report (PDF).

Principles applied throughout:
  a) Conservative by default: the lower of deed and satellite plot area, only
     the approved structure, the lower LTV cap of declared vs observed usage,
     the official government value as a floor, the loan on the lower edge of
     the range when the range is wide.
  b) Every number is traceable: formulas are shown with the numbers
     substituted; every data point keeps its source link and the exact text
     it was copied from; every government rate keeps its locality,
     classification and effective date.
  c) Uncertainty is shown, not hidden: every rate carries an 80% range, and
     a single data point can no longer look certain.
  d) Deterministic: the same inputs always give the same value.
  e) AI only reads and copies printed text (search results, Index II). It
     never estimates, converts or calculates.
  f) No repeat KYC: identity is verified at loan onboarding. The video
     inspection proves the photos were taken AT THE PLEDGED PROPERTY.

3. STEP 1 - APPLICATION, PROPERTY FACTS, PURCHASE PRICE, DOCUMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Property types and how each is valued

  Type                              Default usage  Default structure  Method
  --------------------------------- -------------- ------------------ ----------------------
  Independent house / bungalow      Residential    RCC                Land + Building (cost)
  Flat / apartment                  Residential    RCC                Sales comparison
  Open plot                         Residential    -                  Land only
  Shop / office unit                Commercial     RCC                Sales comparison
  Commercial building (with land)   Commercial     RCC                Land + Building (cost)
  Factory / warehouse / shed        Industrial     Steel / PEB shed   Land + Building (cost)

  Usage: Residential, Commercial, Mixed or Industrial.
  Structure: RCC framed, load-bearing masonry, or steel / PEB shed.

3.2 Facts entered by the bank (Identification -> Facts)
  Address and pin, deed plot area, declared (deed) built-up area, floors,
  age of the building, structure, construction rate (Rs./sqft, bank panel
  rate), approved plan facts (approved BUA, approved floors, permissible FSI,
  plan / permit no., authority) and the year the title chain starts.

3.3 Purchase price and claimed value (new; on the new-application form and
    in Identification -> Facts)
  Field                          Notes
  ------------------------------ ----------------------------------------------
  How the customer got it        Purchased / Inherited / Gifted / Self-built.
                                 Only "Purchased" feeds the purchase-price
                                 component (section 10).
  Month and year of purchase     Required if purchased.
  Price paid (Rs.)               Consideration on the sale deed.
  What the price covered         Plot only / Plot + building / Flat or unit
                                 (decides which rate it is compared with).
  Area bought + unit             Only if different from today's area
                                 (sqft, sq yd, sq m).
  Construction after purchase    Yes / no + year (for a plot-only purchase).
  Sale deed document no.         Optional, for later verification.
  Customer's claimed value       Cross-check only (check T20). NEVER used in
                                 the value.

3.4 Document checklist (generated per property type)
  M = mandatory, R = recommended, - = not applicable

  Document                                         House Flat Plot Shop Comm Ind
  ------------------------------------------------ ----- ---- ---- ---- ---- ---
  Registered sale deed (from loan file)              M    M    M    M    M    M
  Chain of title documents (13 years)                M    M    M    M    M    M
  Approved / sanctioned building plan                M    M    -    M    M    M
  Floor plan (floor-wise areas)              [new]   R    R    -    R    R    R
  Approved layout plan                               -    -    M    -    -    -
  Encumbrance certificate (13 years)                 R    R    R    R    R    R
  Land record extract (7/12, Patta, Khatauni)        R    -    R    -    R    R
  Registration extract (Index II or equivalent)      R    R    R    R    R    R
  Latest property tax receipt (customer)             R    R    R    R    R    R
  Latest electricity bill (customer)                 R    R    -    R    R    R
  Occupancy / completion certificate                 R    R    -    R    R    R
  Society NOC / share certificate (customer)         -    R    -    R    -    -

4. STEP 2 - LOCATION, PLOT BOUNDARY AND GEO-FENCE (geofence.ts)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - The bank confirms the property pin (address search with Google Places,
    falling back to OpenStreetMap / Photon) and clicks the plot corners on the
    satellite image.
  - Satellite plot area = polygon area (sq m) x 10.7639.
  - The plot polygon is also the geo-fence for the inspection photos.

Geo-fence rule for each snapshot:
  allowed radius = 25 m + min(GPS accuracy, 25 m)       (25 to 50 m)
  INSIDE   inside the plot polygon, or within the allowed radius of the pin
  NEAR     within 100 m of the pin (re-take advised)
  OUTSIDE  more than 100 m from the pin
  NO GPS   the photo has no location

Overall: FAIL if any snapshot OUTSIDE; WARN if any NEAR / NO GPS or fewer than
4 exterior views INSIDE; PASS otherwise; NO CAPTURE before any snapshot.

5. STEP 3 - VIDEO INSPECTION AND SNAPSHOTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 The call
  - The officer presses "Call customer"; the customer's portal rings. On answer
    the customer allows location first, then the camera (back camera).
  - Peer-to-peer WebRTC video with a data channel; a TURN server is used when
    networks block direct calls.
  - Customer: mute / unmute, front / back camera, end call, call timer.
  - Bank: mute, switch the customer's camera, shutter (or key S), frame grab,
    portrait / landscape, full screen, hang up, typed / quick instructions.
  - The customer's live location streams during the call (distance from the
    pin shown live) and is stored every 15 seconds.

5.2 Snapshots
  - The shutter makes the CUSTOMER'S PHONE take a full-resolution photo,
    tagged with the phone's GPS fix and a SHA-256 fingerprint ("phone
    capture"). Fallback: a frame grabbed from the received video, tagged with
    the last tracked location under 2 minutes old ("bank-side frame").
  - Every snap lands in the SNAP TRAY first. The officer drags each snap onto
    its designated view: Front elevation, Approach road, Left side, Right
    side, Rear side, Electricity meter / name plate, and "Inside - <floor>"
    for each floor; custom views can be added. One photo per view.
  - Only snaps placed on a view count for the geo-fence, the stages and the
    report.

5.3 Observations recorded from the call
  Floors seen, usage seen, occupancy (self-occupied / rented / vacant).
  "Floors seen" is also the floor count used in the satellite area check.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. STEP 4 - MEASUREMENT (measure.ts)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Room-wise measurement (tape / snapshot ruler) has been REMOVED. The building
is measured floor by floor from its floor plan and checked from above.

6.1 Land
  Land area used  A_land = min(deed area, satellite plot polygon area)
                  (either one alone if only one is available)
  Plot variance   = (satellite - deed) / deed x 100   (tolerance +/-10%, T6)

6.2 Floor-wise area from the floor plan
  1. Plan source (recorded by the officer):
       - Government website: the authority's approved plan, with the permit
         number or portal link (file kept in "Approved building plan"), or
       - Uploaded by the customer (file kept in "Floor plan").
     The plan files open beside the table.
  2. The officer types each floor's area as printed on the plan (Ground,
     First, ... ; extra floors such as a basement can be added).
  3. Basis printed on the plan: built-up / plinth (used as is) or carpet
     (x 1.2 to built-up).
       Floor BUA        = printed area x (1.2 if carpet, else 1)
       Plan total BUA   = sum of the floor BUAs

6.3 Independent check from the satellite top view (houses and buildings)
  The officer clicks the corners of the building's ROOF (not the plot) on the
  satellite image.
       Footprint        = roof polygon area (sq m) x 10.7639
       Floors used      = floors seen on the call; else declared floors;
                          else the number of floors on the plan
       Satellite BUA    = footprint x floors used
       Delta            = (plan total - satellite BUA) / satellite BUA
  Negative delta: more is built than the plan shows (possible extension).
  Positive delta: the plan shows more than is visible from above.

  Flats and shops sit inside a larger building, so a top view cannot measure
  the unit: their plan area is checked against the built-up area on the deed
  instead (Delta = (plan - deed) / deed).

6.4 Measurement confidence score (0-100)
  Part          Max  Rule
  ------------- ---- -------------------------------------------------------
  Agreement      70  |delta| <= 5% -> 70;  |delta| >= 30% -> 0;
                     in between 70 x (1 - (|delta| - 5%) / 25%)
  Floor count    15  floors on the plan = floors seen on the call -> 15;
                     not seen but = declared floors -> 8; otherwise 0
                     (units: 15)
  Plan source    15  government copy, verified 15; government copy not yet
                     verified 10; customer upload verified 10; customer
                     upload not verified 5; no source 0
  Band: 80+ HIGH (pass), 50-79 MEDIUM (condition), below 50 LOW (refer for
  physical measurement) - see check T23.

  Example A: Ground 960 + First 960 = 1,920 sqft (built-up, customer upload,
  verified). Roof outline 960 sqft x 2 floors seen = 1,920 sqft.
  Delta 0% -> 70 + 15 + 10 = 95 (HIGH).
  Example B: same plan, roof 1,200 sqft x 2 = 2,400 sqft. Delta -20% ->
  agreement 70 x (1 - 15/25) = 28; + 15 + 10 = 53 (MEDIUM, condition).
  With an unverified customer plan: 28 + 15 + 5 = 48 (LOW, refer).

6.5 Built-up area that is valued (only the approved structure)
  Actual BUA = floor-plan total if entered, else the declared (deed) BUA.
  BUA_valued =
    - no approved plan on record ........... 0 (building not valued)
    - approved, nothing entered yet ........ approved BUA
    - otherwise ............................ min(actual BUA, approved BUA)
  Deviation vs plan = (actual - approved) / approved x 100   (check T7)
  FSI (houses / buildings) = actual BUA / land area           (check T9)


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. STEP 5 - GOVERNMENT VALUE (govValue.ts, lib/live/igrsTelangana.ts)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The government value (guideline / circle / market value for stamp duty) is a
MINIMUM for stamp duty, not a price estimate. It is shown beside the market
rate, used as a FLOOR when it is official, and has weight 0 in the rate.

7.1 Which properties get the Telangana lookup
  A property is treated as Telangana if its recorded state says Telangana;
  if no state is recorded, if the address mentions Telangana, Hyderabad or
  Secunderabad, or carries a PIN code 50xxxx. Only these cases show the IGRS
  Market Value Search card.

7.2 Telangana IGRS Market Value Search (registration.telangana.gov.in)
  Public, no login, no captcha. Read live by the server and parsed in code:
    1. Search by: Land value (Rs. per sq yd) - plots, houses, buildings -
       or Apartment value (Rs. per sqft) - flats, shops.
    2. District -> Mandal -> Village / town (pre-selected from the address
       where the names match). Hyderabad district: Division + at least 3
       letters of the locality, then the matching locality.
    3. "Get market values" lists every row: locality, ward-block, land value
       per sq yd (or apartment value for ground / first / other floors),
       classification (01 Residential / 02 Commercial) and effective date.
       Rows matching the declared usage are highlighted.
    4. The officer clicks "Use" on the row for the property's street /
       locality. The server reads the row again (from its cache or the
       portal) - the stored rate always comes from the portal, never from the
       browser - and saves it as the case's OFFICIAL government value:
          land value  Rs./sqft = Rs. per sq yd / 9
          apartment   Rs./sqft as published (floor chosen by the officer)
       One land value and one apartment value per case; a new "Use"
       replaces the old one. Official values are kept when the market
       search is run again.
  Lists and rates are cached for 30 days (rates change only on official
  revision); the read date is shown.
  Example: Kokapet village main roads, 01 (Residential), Rs.27,000 per sq yd,
  effective 05/06/2026 -> Rs.3,000 per sqft.

7.3 Other states
  The card shows a link to the official portal where one is known
  (Maharashtra IGR ready reckoner, Karnataka Kaveri, Tamil Nadu TNREGINET,
  Andhra Pradesh IGRS, Gujarat GARVI, Uttarakhand, Uttar Pradesh IGRS, Delhi
  revenue department). The officer may enter the rate as a manual
  "Guideline" point. For any other state the card is not shown.
  Outside Telangana the guideline rate found by the web search (median of the
  guideline points) is used for the T14 check only - NOT as a floor.

8. STEP 6 - MARKET EVIDENCE (lib/server/market.ts)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8.1 Live search ("Fetch market evidence", Valuation tab)
  Real-time web search (Tavily) around the property's locality / city:
    1) "<land asset> for sale in <locality city> price per sq ft"
    2) "<built asset> for sale in <locality city> price per sq ft"
       Both LIMITED to the five property sites: housing.com, 99acres.com,
       magicbricks.com, nobroker.in, squareyards.com.
    3) "<locality city state> guideline value circle rate per sq ft" (open)
    4) Bank e-auctions, limited to baanknet.com and ibapi.in, city-wide.
  Up to 16 ordinary results plus up to 6 auction pages, 800 characters each.
  Social media and catalogue sites are rejected.

8.2 Extraction (copy only)
  Gemini (gemini-flash-lite-latest, fallback gemini-flash-latest) must COPY
  for each individual property or stated rate: category (land / built /
  guideline), kind (listing / sale / auction / guideline), property type with
  its usage words, price, area (with basis words such as "super built-up" or
  "carpet" when printed), rate, project / society name, locality and date -
  exactly as printed. Locality averages and price trends may be copied with
  their words ("average", "price trend"). Auction pages: the reserve price
  only; "N/A" / indicative prices are skipped.

8.3 Validation (deterministic)
  - Every copied price / area / rate must appear verbatim in its source.
  - Units parsed in code (Cr / Lakh / K; sqft / sq yd / sq m / acre /
    hectare / guntha; Devanagari digits). Rate = printed rate, else
    price / area; Rs.100 - Rs.5,00,000 per sqft.
  - Date provenance:
        PRINTED  a date printed in the source (checked verbatim),
        PAGE     the page's published month,
        ASSUMED  no date at all - the point is scored as 12 months old.
    (Listings are no longer stamped with the current month.)
  - Each rejected result is listed with its reason. Without Gemini a regex
    parser keeps explicit "Rs X / sqft" rates only.

8.4 Location of each point
  Geocoded with OpenStreetMap: the project / society name with its locality
  first (exact), then the locality centre (marked "approx. location").

8.5 Bank-added evidence (kept when the search is run again)
  - Registered sale from Index II (Maharashtra): the valuer downloads the
    Index II from IGR's free search (captcha solved by the valuer; never
    automated), uploads it, Gemini copies the fields, code parses amounts /
    area / date, the valuer confirms every field.
  - Manual comparable (land / built / guideline; sale / listing / auction).
  - The reviewer can tick any point in or out, or delete it.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. STEP 7 - MARKET RATE ENGINE (evidence.ts) - "component B"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Land rates (Rs./sqft of plot) and built rates (Rs./sqft of built-up area) are
computed separately. Government values never enter this rate.

9.1 Cleaning - points pass these steps in order; each dropped point is
    listed with its reason
   1. Reviewer exclusion.
   2. Usable rate: no rate, or outside Rs.100 - Rs.5,00,000 per sqft.
   3. Area basis (flats and shops only): a super built-up rate x 1.25, a
      carpet rate / 1.2, built-up as is. Basis not printed: assumed super
      built-up (x 1.25) and tau + 0.02. Manual entries are taken as built-up.
   4. Usage match: comparables must share the subject's usage (a mixed-use
      subject accepts residential and commercial). Usage is read from the
      printed property type; unknown usage is kept.
   5. Type match (built rates): flat / independent house / shop or office /
      commercial building / industrial shed must match the subject type;
      unknown type is kept.
   6. Date provenance (section 8.3); assumed dates count as 12 months.
   7. Granularity: a listing whose copied text says "average", "avg" or
      "price trend" is a LOCALITY AVERAGE and weighs as 3 points.
   8. Duplicates: same link and rate; same registration document number; or
      same locality with rate within 1% AND area within 2%.
   9. Distance: more than 10 km from the pin is dropped (unknown is kept as
      tier T3). Bank auctions are used only in tiers T1 / T2.
  10. Registered sales declared AT the government value (consideration
      within +/-5% of the government value on the same deed) are kept out of
      the rate - they do not show the real price.
  11. Outliers per category + kind (4+ points), on the log of the corrected
      rate:  |ln r - median(ln r)| > 3 x 1.4826 x MAD(ln r)  -> dropped.
  12. Misread band: below 30% or above 300% of the category median (3+).

9.2 Corrections to a closing price, and systematic error tau
  Kind                       Correction                     tau
  -------------------------- ------------------------------ -----
  Registered / reported sale x k_reg (1.00)                  0.05
  Portal listing             x (1 - 0.14) = x 0.86           0.06
  Bank auction (reserve)     / 0.80                          0.10
  tau add-ons, +0.02 each: area basis not printed; no guideline adjustment
  (tier T2 / T3); no price index (sale / auction older than 6 months, or
  commercial / industrial older than 12 months).
  Time adjustment with the RBI House Price Index is built in
  (r_today = r_then x HPI_latest / HPI_quarter_of_sale) but the index table
  is not loaded yet, so the tau add-on applies instead.
  Location adjustment for T2 / T3 points: x G_subject / G_comparable,
  ratio capped 0.67-1.5 (outside = a different market, dropped). It applies
  only when both guideline rates are known.

9.3 Locality tiers and point weights
  Tier  Which points                                          Tier weight
  ----- ----------------------------------------------------- -----------
  T1    same locality name as the subject, or within 1.5 km   1.00
  T2    within 5 km                                           0.50
  T3    within 10 km, or distance unknown                     0.25
  Point weight  w = tier weight x 0.5^(age in months / 12) x (3 if locality
                average, else 1)   - halves every 12 months.
  Effective number of points  n_eff = (sum w)^2 / sum w^2

9.4 Estimate per source kind k (sale, listing, auction)
  r_k    = WEIGHTED MEDIAN of the corrected rates
  s_k    = relative spread:
             4+ points  : 1.4826 x weighted MAD / r_k        (robust)
             2-3 points : SAMPLE standard deviation (n - 1) / mean
             1 point    : standard error taken as 15%
  SE_k   = 1.25 x s_k / sqrt(n_eff,k)      (1.25 = median's efficiency)
  tau_k  = weighted mean tau of its points
  sigma_k = r_k x sqrt(SE_k^2 + tau_k^2)

9.5 Consensus across kinds
  R = sum(r_k / sigma_k^2) / sum(1 / sigma_k^2)
  u = 1 / sqrt(sum(1 / sigma_k^2))                (as a share of R)
  Disagreement test  Q = sum((r_k - R)^2 / sigma_k^2). With K kinds, if
  Q > K - 1 the uncertainty is widened by sqrt(Q / (K - 1)): when sales and
  listings point to different rates the range grows instead of hiding it.

9.6 Local rate blended with the wider rate
  R and u are computed twice: T1 points only (R_T1, u_T1) and all tiers
  (R_all, u_all).
     n_eff(T1) = sum over kinds of n_eff,k of the T1 points
     Z       = n_eff(T1) / (n_eff(T1) + 5)
     R_market = Z x R_T1 + (1 - Z) x R_all       (rounded to Rs.10)
     u_market = Z x u_T1 + (1 - Z) x u_all
  No T1 points: Z = 0.
  80% range = R_market +/- 1.28 x u_market x R_market
  Thin market: n_eff of T1 + T2 below 2 (check T13 refers the case).

9.7 Worked example (Valuation Logic v2 doc, verified by automated test)
  T1 sales 4,900 / 5,150 / 5,300 (6, 14, 20 months old): weighted median
  5,150, n_eff 2.7, sample-SD spread 3.95%, SE 3.0%, sigma 5.8%.
  T1 listings 6,200 / 6,000 / 6,500 x 0.86: r = 5,332, n_eff 3.0, sigma 6.7%.
  R_T1 = 5,226, u 4.4%, Q = 0.15 (kinds agree). n_eff(T1) = 5.7 -> Z = 0.53.
  With the wider points added: R about 5,230, range about 4,940 - 5,520.
  One listing alone: u = sqrt(0.15^2 + 0.06^2) = 16.2% -> range about +/-21%
  and Agreement about 10 (v1 showed a zero-width range and full Agreement).

9.8 Registered-sale and auction cross-checks (not part of R)
  - Registered sales carrying both values: share declared below the
    government value, and share declared at it (check T17).
  - Auction benchmark: median reserve of auctions within 10 km vs PRAMAAN's
    forced-sale rate (80% x rate); below 70% "review the FSV", above 130%
    "FSV looks conservative".


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. STEP 8 - FINAL RATE: MARKET + PURCHASE PRICE + GOVERNMENT FLOOR
    (purchase.ts)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A. Government value   official (IGRS) only: floor, weight 0
  B. Market rate        R_market, u_market (section 9)
  C. Purchase price     the customer's own purchase brought to today

10.1 Component C (only if acquired by purchase, with month, price and what
     the price covered)
  years     = months since the purchase month / 12
  index     = HPI_now / HPI_purchase if the city's index is loaded, else
              (1 + 6%)^years   (growth rate to be calibrated)
  Plot only        -> land rate:  R_C = price x index / area
                      (area bought, else deed plot area)
  Flat / unit      -> built rate: R_C = price x index / area
                      (area bought, else deed built-up area)
  Plot + building  -> land rate:  the building as it was at purchase is
                      taken out:
                      building_then = BUA x construction rate x (1 - D_then),
                      D_then = depreciation at (today's age - years)
                      R_C = (price x index - building_then) / plot area
  u_C = min(30%, 8% + 2% x years) + 5% while self-declared (the 5% is
        dropped once the sale deed is verified)
  C must match the property: a plot purchase is not compared with a flat
  rate and vice versa; if inputs are missing the reason is shown (T21
  pending).

10.2 Blend
  w_C = (1/u_C^2) / (1/u_B^2 + 1/u_C^2), capped at 30%;   w_B = 1 - w_C
  R   = w_B x R_market + w_C x R_C
  u   = sqrt((w_B x u_B)^2 + (w_C x u_C)^2)
  Floor: if an OFFICIAL government value exists and R is below it, R = G.
  Final rate rounded to Rs.10; 80% range = R +/- 1.28 x u x R.
  Divergence: |R_C - R_market| / R_market above 30% -> check T21 warns.

10.3 Worked examples
  Doc example (verified by test): R_market 5,332 (u 6.4%); purchase 5.4
  years ago at Rs.4,000/sqft, 7% growth -> R_C 5,764 (u 23.8%).
  Weights 93% / 7%  ->  R = 5,360, range 4,940 - 5,790; floor 2,778 not hit.
  A purchase one year old (u_C 15%) would get about 15% weight.

  Live test case (Kokapet plot, 1,800 sqft, illustrative comparables):
    A  IGRS Rs.27,000 per sq yd -> Rs.3,000/sqft (floor)
    B  market Rs.8,310/sqft, u 7.1%, range 7,560 - 9,070
    C  Rs.72 lakh in June 2021 -> 5.42 yrs x 1.06^yrs -> Rs.5,484/sqft,
       u 23.8%
    Weights 92% / 8% -> rate Rs.8,080/sqft, range 7,380 - 8,790
    MV 1,800 x 8,080 = Rs.1,45,44,000 (range 1.33 - 1.58 Cr);
    max loan 70% = Rs.1,01,80,800. T21 warns: purchase and market 34% apart.

11. STEP 9 - AUTHENTICITY SCORE (0-100, per land and built market rate)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
How far the market rate can be trusted. Same four parts and maximums as
before, so routing (80 / 60) and check T13 still apply.

  Part       Max  Rule
  ---------- ---- ---------------------------------------------------------
  Agreement   35  from u_market: <= 6% -> 35; >= 20% -> 0; straight line
                  between. Disagreement between kinds is already inside u
                  (through Q).
  Depth       25  20 x min(1, n_eff / 12), plus 5 when at least two
                  independent kinds each have n_eff >= 2 (e.g. registered
                  sales and listings)
  Recency     20  weighted median age: <= 3 months -> 20; >= 24 -> 0;
                  assumed dates count as 12 months
  Spatial     20  20 x (1.0 x weight share in T1 + 0.6 x share in T2 +
                  0.2 x share in T3)
  Doc example: Agreement 35 (u 4.3%), Depth 20 x 8.0/12 + 5 = 18.3, Recency
  about 17, Spatial 20 x (0.64 + 0.6 x 0.36) = 17.1 -> about 87.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
12. STEP 10 - VALUATION METHODS, VALUE RANGE AND LOAN (valuation.ts)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
12.1 Residual life and depreciation
  Economic life: RCC 60, load-bearing 50, steel shed 30 years.
  Residual life = economic life - age
  D = min(85%, age / economic life x (1 - 10% salvage))

12.2 Methods (rates are the FINAL rates of section 10)
  Land + Building (house, commercial building, industrial):
     LV = A_land x R_land;  BV = BUA_valued x construction rate x (1 - D)
     MV = LV + BV                                         <- adopted
     Cross-check MV_B = BUA_valued x R_built; variance > 20% flagged.
  Sales comparison (flat, shop / office):  MV = BUA_valued x R_built
  Land only (open plot):                    MV = A_land x R_land

12.3 Value range
  The 80% rate range is carried into MV. For the cost method only the land
  part moves; the depreciated building value stays fixed.

12.4 Forced-sale value and loan
  FSV = 80% x MV
  Max loan = LTV cap x MV; LTV caps (illustrative): Residential 70%,
  Commercial 60%, Mixed 55%, Industrial 50%; the LOWER cap of declared and
  observed usage is applied.
  Wide-range rule: if u of the rate is above 15%, the max loan is sized on
  the LOWER edge of the MV range. MV in the report stays the central value.
  The final sanction is the lower of this and the cash-flow limit (outside
  PRAMAAN).

18. KNOWN LIMITATIONS AND WHAT IS NOT BUILT YET
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Constants are starting points, not calibrated bank policy (listing
     discount, tau, tiers, growth rate, confidence bands, LTV caps).
  2. The RBI House Price Index table is empty: old sales are not
     time-adjusted (they carry tau + 0.02) and the purchase price grows at a
     flat 6% a year.
  3. Guideline rates of comparable localities are not collected, so the
     location adjustment for T2 / T3 points rarely applies and those points
     carry tau + 0.02.
  4. Distances are to the project or locality centre named in the source,
     not to the exact listed property.
  5. Telangana registered sales (Encumbrance Search after officer login) are
     Phase 2 and not built; Index II (Maharashtra) is entered by the valuer.
     The IGRS parser depends on the portal's current page layout; if it
     changes, the officer uses the portal link and enters the rate manually.
  6. Not built yet: thin-market guideline-ratio fallback, T19 (market-to-
     guideline ratio vs district median), T22 (deed vs EC vs site
     boundaries).
  7. Satellite check: the roof outline includes balconies and overhangs;
     buildings with smaller upper floors, trees or shade over the roof, and
     an unrecorded floor count all widen the delta. Flats and shops are
     checked against the deed area only.
  8. The purchase price and claimed value are self-declared until the sale
     deed is verified.
  9. Demolition lists and road-widening plans have no connected data source;
     the bank attests them (T10, T11).
