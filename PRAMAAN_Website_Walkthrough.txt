================================================================================
 1. THE PROBLEM PRAMAAN SOLVES
================================================================================

 Loan Against Property (LAP) is one of India's fastest-growing secured lending segments
 (industry book ~Rs. 15.95 Lakh Crore, +20% YoY), serving mainly MSMEs. Every loan depends on
 an accurate collateral valuation, but that valuation is still done manually by third-party
 agencies through a physical site visit.

 What that costs IDFC FIRST Bank today:
   * Turnaround time (TAT): 5-7 days typical; the 3 real IDFC sample reports took 10 days (R1)
     and 18 days (R3) from site visit to e-signature.
   * Valuation is the main bottleneck: once credit is decided, disbursal itself takes 2-3 hours.
   * Cost: ~Rs. 3,000 + GST per file to the agency, plus rework.
   * Quality: unrecorded "local enquiry" pricing with zero comparable evidence, and human errors
     that slip through (the IDFC samples contain wrong amount-in-words, survey-number mismatches,
     contradictory dates, and a 52.68% building deviation rated "Low" demolition risk).

 Where the 7 days actually go (from the PRD's failure analysis):
   1. Case & document collection ......... Day 1 (1-3 days of chasing missing documents)
   2. Valuer allocation & scheduling ..... Day 1-2
   3. Physical site visit ................ Day 2-3 (only ~0.5 day — less than 10% of elapsed time)
   4. Plan vs actual & deviations ........ Day 3-4
   5. Rate & comparables ................. Day 4-5 (broker phone calls, no audit trail)
   6. Drafting, QC, e-sign & queries ..... Day 5-7 (up to 18 days)

 Key insight: the site visit is a tiny part of the delay. ~70-80% of report fields already exist in
 government/public databases or uploaded documents, and the valuation arithmetic is deterministic.
 Only the market rate genuinely needs triangulated evidence.

 What IDFC officials asked for:
   * Speed: 30 minutes to 1 hour; 24 hours maximum for exceptions.
   * "Zero or One": replace the physical visit and manual work — not just a helper app for valuers.
   * Three hard pillars: (1) Measure without visiting, (2) Price without asking anyone,
     (3) Read property usage from photos.
   * Authenticity: "prices exist online, but price authenticity does not" — controls must match or
     beat physical underwriting.


================================================================================
 2. THE CORE IDEA IN ONE PARAGRAPH
================================================================================

 The property owner (or an authorised representative) captures the property on their own phone in
 about 15 minutes, guided step by step and secured by an anti-fraud Trust Layer. Documents are
 read by OCR (including Tamil and Hindi). Three deterministic engines then MEASURE the property,
 PRICE it from 6 independent data sources with an auditable Authenticity Score, and confirm its
 USAGE. A Valuation engine computes Market Value, Forced-Sale Value and the loan limit; Auto-QC
 checks the report for the kinds of errors humans make; and a Risk Cascade routes the file to
 INSTANT, REMOTE REVIEW or PHYSICAL CHECK based on how confident the evidence is. A registered
 valuer reviews the evidence pack remotely and e-signs — so the bank stays RBI-compliant with zero
 field visits on Day 1. Total: 30-55 minutes instead of 7-18 days.

 The non-negotiable design rule: AI is used only to READ and CLASSIFY (documents, photos). Every
 number — area, deviation, rate, score, value, route — is computed by plain TypeScript formulas in
 lib/engines/. No AI ever does financial arithmetic, so every figure is reproducible and auditable.

 3. ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

   Owner phone capture ─┐
   Documents (OCR) ─────┼─> TRUST LAYER ─> ENGINE 1 MEASURE ─> ENGINE 3 USAGE ─> ENGINE 2 PRICE
   Satellite / GIS ─────┘                                                              │
                                                                                       v
          REMOTE VALUER e-SIGN  <── RISK CASCADE  <── AUTO-QC (6 rules)  <── VALUATION CALCULATOR

 TRUST LAYER (lib/engines/trust.ts) — proves the capture happened at the right parcel, live, by the
 right person, without omissions:
   1. GPS-in-Parcel: capture point must be inside the parcel polygon (or within 25 m of centroid).
   2. Live camera only + device attestation: blocks gallery uploads, rooted phones, mock-GPS apps.
   3. Image forensics + sun-angle check: shadows must match the sun's position for that GPS + time.
   4. Identity: selfie face-match to KYC (or to a registered representative for NRI/tenant cases).
   5. Floor Coverage Lock: if floors walked inside < floors seen outside -> OMISSION_FRAUD_BLOCK.
   6. Hindsight audit sampling: 3-5% of Instant files are flagged for post-disbursal physical audit.

 ENGINE 1 — MEASURE (lib/engines/measure.ts)
   * Net plot area A_net = min(deed area, record area) - road-widening loss
   * Multi-source area reconciliation; flag if any source differs > 10%
   * BUA deviation % = (actual - sanctioned) / sanctioned x 100 (tin sheds reported separately)
   * Building area actually valued depends on plan policy:
       plan exists -> min(actual, sanctioned); no plan + land-only policy -> 0;
       no plan + institutional exception -> actual (flagged)
   * Height from satellite shadow (floors = height / 3 m); known-object scaling (7 ft door)
   * 4-direction boundary check (deed vs satellite) and GIS hazard overlays
     (forest buffer, road widening, CRZ/waterbody, HT line, caution/negative areas)

 ENGINE 2 — PRICE & AUTHENTICITY (lib/engines/price.ts)
   Six sources, each with its known bias corrected:
     Registered sale deeds  -> x k_reg (deeds are under-declared near circle rate)
     Portal listings        -> x (1 - d) (asking price > closing price; de-duplicated)
     Past IDFC reports      -> x HPI_now / HPI_then (indexed to today)
     Bank auctions          -> / 0.80 (distressed prices lifted to market)
     RERA / builder lists   -> - net offer value
     Circle / guideline rate-> weight 0% (sanity floor only; e.g. 7.82x below market in R3)
   * Consensus rate = weighted average of corrected rates, with a confidence range
   * AUTHENTICITY SCORE S (0-100) = Agreement (/35) + Comps Depth (/25) + Recency (/20)
     + Spatial Typicality (/20). Thin markets fall back 1 km -> Ward -> SRO -> District with a
     transparent penalty, so weak evidence automatically routes to human review.
   * Drift Guard: if hindsight audits show > 7% error in a locality, the Instant bar rises 80 -> 88.

 ENGINE 3 — USAGE (lib/engines/usage.ts)
   * Computer-vision usage from photos (kitchen = residential, signage/counters = commercial, ...)
     cross-checked against records (EB tariff, property-tax class, zoning).
   * LTV caps: Residential 70% > Commercial 60% > Mixed 55% > Industrial / Institutional 50%.
   * Conflict rule: a conflict NEVER resolves upward — the lower LTV band is applied and the file
     escalates.

 VALUATION CALCULATOR (lib/engines/valuation.ts)
   * Land + Building: Land = A_net x rate; Building = BUA_valued x construction rate (depreciated:
     D = min(0.85, age / total life x 0.90)); MV = Land + Building + Amenities
   * Comparable-sales method (RERA flats) and Income-capitalisation cross-check (leased commercial:
     flag if income value differs > 15% from MV)
   * FSV = MV x 0.80-0.85; Max Collateral Loan = LTV cap x MV
   * Exact Indian Rupee words converter (Crore / Lakh / Thousand) — and a reverse parser used by QC

 AUTO-QC (lib/engines/autoQc.ts) — 6 rules run on every report:
   QC-1 Words vs figures · QC-2 Date chronology & TAT · QC-3 Deviation vs demolition risk ·
   QC-4 Arithmetic & FSV recalculation · QC-5 Internal status contradictions ·
   QC-6 Cross-document survey numbers & sanctioned-map geo-tag (> 25 m = fail)

 RISK CASCADE (lib/engines/routing.ts)
   INSTANT (30-55 min) — ALL of: S >= 80; Trust 100% pass; area within 10% and BUA deviation
     <= 15%; usage confirmed by 2 sources; sanctioned plan, no survey/title mismatch, no critical
     QC fail; MV <= Rs. 5 Cr.
   REMOTE REVIEW (same day) — e.g. S 60-79, missing plan, survey mismatch, road widening / forest
     review, irregular plot, yield variance > 15%, MV Rs. 5-15 Cr.
   PHYSICAL CHECK (24-72 hrs) — e.g. S < 60, BUA deviation > 30% or unapproved floors/sheds, Trust
     failure, severe usage conflict, commercial > Rs. 15 Cr with major deviation (dual valuation).

 MASTER ORCHESTRATOR (lib/engines/evaluateCase.ts) runs everything in order and returns one
 "Evidence Pack" that every screen and the PDF read from.

 4. WEBSITE FLOW, SCREEN BY SCREEN
================================================================================

 The maroon top bar (IDFC brand colour #9C1D26) has 4 tabs and a "Demo Mode" pill:
   Portfolio Command Center · Owner Self-Capture · Underwriter Workbench · IDFC Report & e-Sign
 (Workbench and Report become active once a case is opened.)

 the engines. "Reset demo" on Tab 1 restores the 4 original cases.

 ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 4.1  TAB 1 — PORTFOLIO COMMAND CENTER   (https://pramaan-idfc.vercel.app/)
 ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Purpose: the credit head's view of the whole valuation pipeline.

 a) Five KPI cards (headline figures from the PRD's portfolio targets; the small grey line under
    each is computed live from the cases in the queue):
      Median TAT 41 min vs 7 days · Instant Route Share 68% · Avg Authenticity Score 84/100 ·
      Legacy QC Errors Caught 11 across 3 IDFC samples · Cost per File Rs. 480 vs Rs. 3,000+

 b) Case Queue table — every case with: IDFC R1/R2/R3 or SHOWCASE tag, file & case ref, applicant,
    Market Value (Rs. Cr and full Indian-format figure), Authenticity Score pill (green >= 80,
    amber 60-79, red < 60), Auto-QC badge (e.g. "CRITICAL FAIL · 3 issues"), Routing badge with SLA,
    PRAMAAN TAT vs struck-through legacy TAT, and an "Open Workbench" button. Signed cases show
    "SIGNED & READY FOR DISBURSAL".
    Filter pills: All / Instant / Remote Review / Physical Check (with counts).

 c) Header buttons:
      "+ Start Owner Self-Capture"       -> Tab 2
      "Upload Live PDF / Photos (AI)"    -> AI extraction modal (section 4.5)
      "IDFC Business Case / ROI Simulator" -> modal with 9 sliders (files/month, legacy vs PRAMAAN
         cost per file, instant share, avg ticket, baseline conversion, conversion uplift, NIM,
         processing fee) showing Year-1 P&L impact = valuation cost saving + incremental NII +
         fees. Defaults are editable planning assumptions, clearly labelled as such.

 How this solves the problem: gives management one screen showing speed, cost, risk mix and the
 business case — the value of moving from 7 days to under an hour is visible in money terms.

 ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 4.2  TAB 2 — OWNER MOBILE SELF-CAPTURE SIMULATOR   (/capture)
 ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Purpose: replaces valuer scheduling and the site visit. Split screen:
   LEFT  = a realistic 390 px phone frame (what the owner sees)
   RIGHT = "PRAMAAN Trust & Extraction Telemetry Stream" (what the bank sees, in real time)

 Toggle at the top: "Owner App" or "Zero-Install WhatsApp Rep Link" (for NRIs, tenants or
 institutional site representatives — no app install needed; the phone frame re-skins and KYC
 verifies the registered representative instead).

 The 5-step guided flow (each "Continue" unlocks only when its step is genuinely complete):
   Step 1 Consent & KYC — Aadhaar OTP via DigiLocker, PAN check, selfie face-match. Only masked
          placeholders such as [Aadhaar OTP Verified] are ever shown — never real digits.
   Step 2 Address & Parcel Pin — interactive satellite map. Tapping inside the plot boundary turns
          "GPS-in-Parcel" green; toggles show the Master Plan road-widening and forest/hazard
          overlays.
   Step 3 Smart Document Upload — Sale Deed, Sanctioned Plan, EB bill, Property Tax (required) and
          a Hindi land record (optional). Owner name, area and survey number are extracted
          instantly and cross-checked; the deed vs land-record area difference (1,800 vs 1,795
          sqft) is highlighted before the owner leaves the screen.
   Step 4 Guided Capture — approach road width (door-scaled: 30.0 ft), exterior facade (floors
          auto-counted from shadow: 9.2 m -> 3 floors), electricity meter (with a random live
          prompt), then a floor-by-floor interior walkthrough. Every frame is logged with a signed
          hash and a sun-angle check.
   Step 5 Run 3-Engine Pipeline — a 1.5-second animated run (Trust -> Measure -> Usage -> Price ->
          Auto-QC -> Risk Cascade). The new case is saved and its Workbench opens automatically.
          A clean capture routes INSTANT (S 91, MV Rs. 1,75,60,200).

 Telemetry panel (right): 6 live Trust badges (GPS, Live Camera, Device Attestation, Sun-Angle,
 Face Match, Floor Coverage), a live extraction & cross-check table, and a terminal-style event log.

 FRAUD SIMULATION BAR (bottom right) — three one-click demos for the stage:
   (a) "Simulate Omission Fraud (Skip Top Floor)" -> owner skips the top floor; Floor Coverage Lock
       raises OMISSION_FRAUD_BLOCK (exterior 3 floors > interior 2).
   (b) "Simulate Mock GPS (180m Outside Parcel)" -> device position jumps 180 m away (red pin on the
       map); GPS-in-Parcel and Device Attestation fail.
   (c) "Simulate Gallery Upload / AI Photo" -> Live Camera and Sun-Angle forensics fail; frames
       are rejected.
   While any fraud is active, Step 5 shows "Submission halted by Trust Layer". Click the button
   again to clear it.

 How this solves the problem: valuer allocation (Days 1-2) is eliminated and the site visit
 (Days 2-3) becomes a 15-minute owner capture. The Trust Layer answers the obvious objection —
 "won't owners cheat?" — by blocking the three most common frauds live.

 ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 4.3  TAB 3 — UNDERWRITER & REMOTE VALUER WORKBENCH   (/cases/[id])
 ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Purpose: where the underwriter / remote valuer reviews the evidence pack in minutes.

 Sticky Decision Header (always visible): case ref, title, Market Value, FSV, Max Collateral Loan at
 the confirmed LTV cap, the Routing pill (click it to expand the exact pass/fail checklist of the 6
 Instant gates plus every Remote/Physical trigger), and "Preview Report & Remote e-Sign".

 EVIDENCE DRAWER: any dotted-underlined figure (MV, FSV, loan, land value, area, deviation %,
 consensus rate, Authenticity Score, each source's corrected rate, yield...) can be clicked. A panel
 slides in showing the exact formula, the raw inputs, the source document citation and an
 audit-replay timestamp. This is the "zero audit trail" problem solved: every number explains
 itself.

 Four sub-tabs:

 A · Engine 1 — Measure & GIS
     * Area Reconciliation table: Sale Deed vs Patta/Cadastral vs Road widening -> Net valued area.
     * Floor-wise Sanctioned (or Permissible) vs Actual BUA bar chart — tin sheds in amber (R2's
       5th-floor tin shed stands out); deviation shown excluding and including temporary sheds.
     * Satellite GIS map: parcel footprint, road-widening strip (amber) and, for R2, a dashed line
       to the sanctioned-map geo-tag 74 m away from the real site. GIS hazard chips above the map.
     * 4-direction E/W/N/S boundary matrix (deed vs site).
     * Vernacular OCR cards: Hindi Forest Division RTI (R1), Tamil Patta + Sale Deed (R3).

 B · Engine 2 — Price & Authenticity (the key "wow" screen)
     * 6-source bias-correction table + bar chart (raw vs corrected, with the consensus line).
     * Authenticity Score radial gauge with the 4 sub-score bars (/35, /25, /20, /20).
     * Comparables map: nearby registered sales, listings, past IDFC reports and auctions as
       colour-coded pins (hover for rate, date, distance), with a table view.
     * LIVE STRESS-TEST & SENSITIVITY PANEL — four sliders:
         (1) Comps count  (2) Source variance / CV %  (3) Spatial fallback tier (1 km -> District)
         (4) Market overstatement x
       They recompute the Score, the Effective LTV (LTV_true = LTV_stated x (1 + x)) and the
       Routing tier live. Example on the clean Nagpur case: x = 5% turns 70% LTV into 73.5% and
       flips INSTANT -> REMOTE REVIEW; cutting comps to 3 at District tier drops S to 54 and flips
       to PHYSICAL CHECK. This shows panelists exactly how the system protects itself when
       evidence is weak.

 C · Engine 3 — Usage & Trust
     * Annotated photo cards with CV detections (e.g. "Residential Kitchen 98%", "Commercial
       Signage 96%", "5th Floor Tin Shed 94%").
     * Dual-source verification card: CV vs utility/tax/zoning records, the LTV cap applied, and
       the rule that conflicts never resolve upward.
     * 6 green/red Trust Layer telemetry badges + the hindsight-audit sampling status.

 D · Auto-QC & Legacy Error Audit
     For each of the 6 rules, side by side: "What the legacy human report missed" vs "What PRAMAAN
     flagged on Day 0", with status badge and required action. (Section 5 lists every catch.)

 How this solves the problem: Steps 4 (plan vs actual), 5 (rate & comps) and most of 6 (QC) take
 minutes instead of days, and every figure has an audit trail — which is what turns online price
 data into AUTHENTICATED price evidence.

 ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 4.4  TAB 4 — AUTO-GENERATED IDFC REPORT, PDF & REMOTE e-SIGN   (/cases/[id]/report)
 ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Purpose: replaces manual drafting and the 9-15 day sign-off lag.

 * A print-layout IDFC FIRST Bank Technical Appraisal Report, auto-filled:
     1 Basic Details · 2 Locality, Geo-coordinates & GIS · 3 Building & Floor-wise Deviation table ·
     4 Land & Building Valuation table with amount in words, FSV, LTV and loan ·
     5 Income Yield Cross-Check (leased commercial) · 6 Boundaries · 7 Auto-QC Remarks ·
     8 Annex: price evidence, geo-coordinates & comparables · 9 Digital Signature Block
 * "Download IDFC PDF Report" — one click produces a clean 4-page PDF (jsPDF + autotable) with the
   maroon header, page numbers, every table, the risk-cascade gates and the signature block.
 * "Remote Registered Valuer e-Sign (RBI Phase 1)" — a 3-step modal:
     1) pick an empanelled valuer and tick the 6-item evidence checklist;
     2) enter the OTP (demo OTP 482613 is shown on screen);
     3) the case is stamped with valuer name, IBBI registration no., timestamp, "Valid 90 Days" and
        a SHA-256 seal of the evidence pack. Status becomes SIGNED & READY FOR DISBURSAL (also shown
        in the Tab 1 queue and in the PDF).
 * Physical Check cases (e.g. R2) cannot be remote-signed; they get "Escalate to Physical
   Inspection" instead — high-risk files never take the remote path.

 How this solves the problem: drafting is zero (the report writes itself from the evidence pack),
 and sign-off goes from 10-18 days to minutes while keeping a registered valuer accountable — the
 RBI independent-valuer requirement is met without a field visit.

 ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 4.5  LIVE AI UPLOAD   (Tab 1 button -> POST /api/analyze-live)
 ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 * Upload a real appraisal PDF and/or property photos (PDF/JPG/PNG/WEBP/HEIC, up to 8 files, 18 MB).
 * With a GEMINI_API_KEY configured, Gemini 2.5 Flash extracts ONLY the values printed in the
   document into a strict JSON schema (it is explicitly told not to calculate anything). A
   sanitiser validates every field and masks any 12-digit ID number.
 * The extracted values are passed through the same deterministic engines; the modal shows the
   extracted fields, the engine's MV / Score / Route, and "Save case & open Workbench".
 * No key, AI failure, or no internet -> "Simulated Extraction Mode": a clearly labelled demo
   extraction, so the stage demo never errors. (The live site currently runs in this mode.)
 * Uploaded reports have no PRAMAAN live capture, so their Trust Layer is marked "not passed" and
   they are priced from a single stated rate — PRAMAAN deliberately routes them conservatively and
   asks for an owner self-capture.

 Case 1 — Clean Showcase · Dharampeth, Nagpur · IDFC-PRM-2026-001  ->  INSTANT (green)

   sanctioned 2,800 = 2.14% deviation (PASS). 56 comparables across 4 sources; consensus
   Rs. 7,560/sqft (circle rate Rs. 2,800 = 2.7x gap); S = 91 (1 km micro-locality).
   Land Rs. 1,80,68,400 + Building Rs. 54,60,000 = MV Rs. 2,35,28,400 (~Rs. 2.35 Cr);
   FSV (85%) Rs. 1,99,99,140; Max loan (70% LTV) Rs. 1,64,69,880.
   All 6 Trust checks PASS, all 6 Auto-QC rules PASS, all 6 Instant gates PASS — 38 minutes,
   ready for 1-click remote e-sign.
   PROVES: the straight-through path works end to end when evidence is strong.

 Case 2 — IDFC Sample Report 1 (R1) · Chandrabadni, Dehradun (residential) · IDFC/UKDDN26051909
   -> REMOTE REVIEW (amber)
   Legacy: Black Olive Ventures, visit 25/05/2026 -> signed 04/06/2026 = 10-day TAT; valuer stated
   Rs. 9,000/sqft with ZERO comparables.
   PRAMAAN: 24 m Master Plan road widening removes 282 sqft (3,831 -> 3,549 sqft net). No
   sanctioned plan -> bank policy values LAND ONLY. BUA 4,278 vs permissible 3,721 = 14.96%.
   52 comparables give a consensus of Rs. 9,060/sqft (S = 84).
   MV Rs. 3,19,41,000 (Rs. 3.19 Cr, at the legacy Rs. 9,000 baseline; PRAMAAN consensus value
   Rs. 3,21,53,940 shown alongside); FSV (80%) Rs. 2,55,52,800.
   Catches: QC-5 FAIL — Stage of Construction "100%" while remarks say "under-construction
   property"; QC-2 WARN — 10-day sign-off lag; plus missing plan, road widening, irregular plot and
   forest-buffer review (Hindi Forest Division RTI read by vernacular OCR confirms Khasra 2206 &
   2221 are not forest land).
   PROVES: PRAMAAN backs the valuer's rate with 52 comparables, and correctly sends policy
   exceptions to a same-day human review instead of auto-approving them.

 Case 3 — IDFC Sample Report 2 (R2) · RS Tower, Rajpur Road, Dehradun (commercial)
   · IDFC/UKDDN26061359  ->  PHYSICAL CHECK (red)
   Leased to IDFC FIRST Bank itself at Rs. 16,00,000/month. Net plot 4,139 sqft after 33 m road
   widening. Sanctioned BUA 13,611 sqft vs actual 20,782 sqft RCC = 52.68% deviation (81.85%
   including a 3,970 sqft 5th-floor tin shed).

   -> MV Rs. 20,10,60,000 (Rs. 20.11 Cr); FSV Rs. 16,08,48,000; gross rental yield 9.55% (income
   value within 15% of MV); S = 74.
   Catches: QC-3 CRITICAL FAIL — 52.68% deviation + unapproved tin shed rated "Low" demolition risk

   real site. Policy gate: commercial > Rs. 15 Cr with > 30% deviation -> dual valuation &
   physical audit. Remote e-sign is disabled for this case.
   PROVES: PRAMAAN is not a rubber stamp — it catches what a human missed and escalates high-risk,
   high-ticket assets.

 Case 4 — IDFC Sample Report 3 (R3) · NGGO Colony, Thudiyalur, Coimbatore (residential)
   · ADR/CBE/July/26/IDFC/022  ->  REMOTE REVIEW (amber, hold for Rectification Deed)

     QC-6 FAIL — Sale Deed lists only SF 173A/1F; the Tamil Patta lists 165/21, 173A/1A, 174A/1F

================================================================================
 7. HOW PRAMAAN MEETS THE BANK'S RISK, AUTHENTICITY AND REGULATORY BAR
================================================================================

 * Price authenticity, not just price: every rate is bias-corrected, weighted, scored 0-100 and
   traceable to its sources. Low agreement, few comps, stale data or a thin market lower S, and a
   low S automatically forces human review.
 * Deterministic and auditable: all math lives in lib/engines/ as pure TypeScript. The same inputs
   always give the same outputs; `npm run verify` checks 60 exact figures and behaviours.
 * Confidence-gated automation: only files that pass ALL 6 gates take the Instant path; everything
   else goes to Remote Review (same day) or Physical Check (24-72 hrs).
 * Conservative by construction: usage conflicts take the lower LTV; Physical Check files cannot be
   remote-signed; uploaded reports without a live capture are not trusted.
 * Fraud resistance: GPS-in-parcel, live-camera-only, device attestation, sun-angle forensics, face
   match and the Floor Coverage Lock — each demonstrable live on Tab 2.
 * RBI compliance (Phase 1): AI does 100% of measurement, pricing, usage, QC and drafting; an
   empanelled registered valuer audits the evidence pack and e-signs remotely — zero field visits,
   zero manual drafting, independent valuation preserved.
 * Continuous learning: hindsight audits of 3-5% of Instant files feed the Drift Guard, which
   tightens the Instant threshold (80 -> 88) in any locality where errors exceed 7%.
 * Works offline: all calculations, charts, PDF export and e-sign run in the browser; if the venue
   Wi-Fi drops, only the map tiles fall back to a styled placeholder with an "Offline" notice.

 Business impact (PRD Section 1.5, for the pitch):
   * Valuation opex: Rs. 6-13 Cr/year direct savings for IDFC in Year 1 (Rs. ~480 vs Rs. 3,000+ per
     file on 65% of files).
   * Conversion: cutting TAT from 5-7 days to < 1 hour reduces MSME attrition to faster NBFCs;
     a 2-3% pull-through lift = Rs. 800-1,200 Cr/year incremental LAP disbursals.
   * Portfolio monitoring: monthly automated revaluation of the existing LAP book at near-zero cost.

================================================================================
 10. HONEST NOTES — WHAT IS REAL, SIMULATED OR CALIBRATED
================================================================================

  Real and exact
   * Every figure stated in the PRD for Case 1, R1, R2 and R3 (areas, rates, MV, FSV, deviation,
     yield, scores, routes and the QC catches) is reproduced exactly by the engines and asserted
     by `npm run verify`.
   * All formulas follow PRD Section 3; the PDF, maps, charts, stress test and fraud blocks run
     on those real engine outputs.

  Simulated for the prototype
   * KYC, OTP, face match, device attestation, camera frames and document uploads in Tab 2 are
     simulated; no real Aadhaar/PAN data is ever displayed.
   * Photo cards in Tab 3-C are placeholder graphics with annotated detections (no real photos).
   * The e-sign is a demo flow with fictional valuers (IBBI/RV/DEMO/...) and a SHA-256 seal; it is
     not a legally valid digital signature.
   * The live site has no Gemini key, so AI upload runs in Simulated Extraction Mode.
   * Map parcel shapes are illustrative squares sized to the deed area; R2's 74 m offset direction
     is not stated in the report and is drawn to the north-east.

  Calibrated (values the PRD does not state, chosen so the engines reproduce the PRD's outputs)
   * PRAMAAN TAT for R1/R2/R3 (43, 49, 39 min), CV confidence for R1/R2/R3, source data ages and
     spatial tiers, and the full price-source breakdown for R2 and R3 (the PRD gives only their
     final rate and score). These are marked "CALIBRATED" in lib/data/seedCases.ts.
   * KPI headline numbers on Tab 1 are the PRD's portfolio-level targets; the live sub-lines under
     each card are computed from the 4-5 demo cases and naturally differ.
   * Stress-test policy (LTV_true above the cap -> Remote; 10+ points above -> Physical) and the
     ROI simulator defaults are prototype assumptions, labelled as such in the app.
